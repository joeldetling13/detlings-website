Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a Full Detail take?

Most of a day, depending on vehicle size, condition, and the exterior level you choose. You'll get a time estimate with your quote — and you don't need to wait around while we work.

Do I need to be home?

No — as long as we can access the car and there's space to set up, you can go about your day.

Why is the interior always the Signature deep clean?

Because 'full detail' should mean full results. The Express interior is a maintenance clean — great on its own, but not what people picture when they book the works. We'd rather over-deliver.
