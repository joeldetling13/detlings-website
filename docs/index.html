Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How much does mobile detailing cost in Cincinnati?

Our exterior detail starts at $89. Our most popular service, the Signature interior deep clean, starts at $259. Larger vehicles and heavier messes add to the price. Tap "Get Quote" and fill out the form with your vehicle and what it needs, and you'll get a straight quote — no upsells, no surprises.

Do you really come to me?

Yes. We bring everything to your home or workplace anywhere in Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky — you don't drop off your car or wait at a shop. Most customers hand us the keys and get on with their day.

What do you need from me on-site?

A place to park the vehicle, plus access to an outdoor water spigot and a standard power outlet — we bring everything else. A driveway, garage, or your own parking spot all work. If you're not sure whether your location has water and power access, just text us and we'll sort it out.

How long does a detail take?

An Express interior or The Wash is quick; a Signature deep clean or full Exterior Detail takes several hours. You'll get a time estimate with your quote.

What areas do you serve?

All of Greater Cincinnati and Northern Kentucky, including Mason, West Chester, and surrounding communities. See the full list on our service areas page.

Can you remove pet hair and stains?

Yes. Seat and carpet shampoo and stain treatment are part of our interior detail, and pet hair removal is available as an add-on — it takes real extra time to do right, so we price it separately. Mention pets when you request your quote and we'll include it up front, no surprises on the day.
